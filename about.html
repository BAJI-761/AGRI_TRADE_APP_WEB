FARMING WITH DIGNITY

Bridging the Rural Digital Divide

AgriTrade was born out of a critical mission: while urban commerce enjoys frictionless, direct, and automated digital systems, India's 150 million farming families remain bound to an archaic, multi-layered middleman broker network.

By coordinating native mobile architectures (Flutter), secure serverless cloud infrastructure (Firestore), and cutting-edge large language model voice parsing (Gemini AI API), we built a platform requiring zero typing skills and offering absolute financial transparency.

Our goal is to shift price-setting power from brokers back to soil operators, improving farmer margins by 20-40% and drastically reducing post-harvest supply chain waste in India's agrarian heartlands.
