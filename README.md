## 🚀 Overview
**AgriTrade** is a voice-powered B2B agricultural platform designed to eliminate middlemen margins and empower India's farming communities. By integrating AI-powered soil diagnostics, multilingual voice accessibility, and transparent peer-to-peer trading markets, AgriTrade puts control back into the hands of farmers.

## ✨ Core Features
- 🎙️ **Multilingual Voice Access**: Built-in Gemini voice-intent decoders to support rural farmers bypassing typing requirements.
- 🌍 **AI Soil Diagnostics**: Precision agronomy leveraging Sentinel-2 Satellite APIs to extract NDVI values and localized soil chemical data.
- 🤝 **Peer-to-Peer B2B Markets**: Direct-to-retailer trade markets utilizing secure Razorpay digital escrow webhooks and UPI settling rails.
- 🚚 **Shared Cooperative Freight**: Consolidates separate listings to dispatch high-capacity freight trucks, reducing cargo travel costs significantly.
- 🏦 **Micro-Credit Indices**: Generates transparent credit indices based on escrow settlement records to enable low-interest cooperative crop loans.
