Frictionless Farm Journeys

Explore the full logical flow of the AgriTrade Flutter application. Interact with the phases and steps below to inspect real-time mobile emulations.

Step 01

Splash App Entry

The initial splash app entry compiling all modular Flutter assets and Lottie animations on load.

Step 02

Language Selection

With one simple tap, the entire application layout, acoustic voice models, and interfaces toggle instantly between regional Telugu and English.

Step 03

Twilio OTP Sign-up

Password-free credentials protect rural user databases. Secure, single-use OTP codes verify phone numbers within seconds.

Step 04

Persona Configuration

Define account classification to isolate workflows: Farmers get crop diagnostic engines, and Retailers get bulk B2B inventory streams.

Step 05

Diagnostic Inputs

Farmers enter localized soil classification parameters (Red, Black, Clay, Loamy) alongside regional water supply levels and current budget constraints.

Step 06

AI Suitability Matching

Advanced algorithms weigh soil and water vectors to rank matching crop types with exact confidence percentages.

Step 07

Crop Suitability Details

Generative AI reports analyze chemical soil parameters to display predicted yield weights, projected rates, and detailed crop care tips.

Step 08

Multilingual Voice Commands

Farmers speak naturally in regional Telugu or English accents to execute bids, request soil advice, or explore buyers completely hands-free.

Step 09

Publish Crop Payloads

Farmers instantly upload active harvest weight listings, adding visual proof and price expectations directly into the shared B2B mandi directory.

Step 10

Real-Time Bids Ledger

Regional wholesalers, supermarkets, and hotels inspect harvest payloads and submit direct, competitive price bids on active listings.

Step 11

Escrow Payment Logs

Negotiated trade funds are secured in Razorpay/UPI digital escrow vaults, protecting farmers from wholesale payment defaults.

Step 12

Direct UPI Settlement

Capital is released directly to the farmer's bank account upon matching dual-QR codes scanned at the logistics pickup gate.
